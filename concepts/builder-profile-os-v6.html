Working method

From customer problem to testable product.

01

Find the gap

Talk to customers and internal teams, inspect the current workflow, and identify where value is leaking.

02

Design the workflow

Define the user, decision path, AI role, data boundaries, and human-control points before building.

03

Build the testable version

Ship the smallest credible product, label its limits, and use feedback to decide what becomes production.
