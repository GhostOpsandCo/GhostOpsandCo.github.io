# Discoverability and Revenue Plan

## Goal

Help qualified people find Elijah Paul, understand the value of his work, and take a paid next step.

The site has three commercial jobs:

1. Help hiring teams evaluate Elijah for AI Solutions Architect, AI Product, and forward-deployed product roles.
2. Help crypto, fintech, and AI companies hire Elijah to find and build a product solution.
3. Help product buyers, partners, and acquirers inspect working products and start a commercial conversation.

Traffic without qualified conversations is not success. The primary scorecard is interviews, consulting inquiries, product conversations, and booked revenue.

## First-Principles Strategy

### 1. Lead with the outcome

Core promise:

> I turn customer problems into working AI products.

Supporting position:

> I find costly workflow and product gaps, design the solution, build the working version, and help teams validate it with customers.

This is broad enough to support full-time and consulting opportunities while remaining specific about the work.

### 2. Prove the promise with inspectable work

Each case study must answer five questions in order:

1. What problem existed?
2. Who had the problem?
3. What did Elijah design and build?
4. What works today?
5. What commercial or product outcome could it create?

Claims must distinguish live behavior from modeled inputs, prototypes, future integrations, and employer-owned work.

### 3. Match one page to one search intent

The site should not ask one page to rank for every service. Its search architecture is:

| Page | Primary intent | Commercial action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | AI solutions architect and product builder | Choose hiring, consulting, or product path |
| Work | AI product builder portfolio | Open relevant proof |
| Treasury Router | AI treasury and institutional advisor workflow | Discuss product or role fit |
| GridSynapse | AI compute optimization and procurement | Inspect product and source |
| Orelis | AI voice agent for service businesses | Inspect commercial product |
| Monarch Doctor | x402 and agent payment developer tooling | Inspect open-source implementation |
| Services | AI product consulting and solutions architecture | Start a scoped build conversation |
| Resume | AI solutions architect resume | Download resume or discuss a role |
| Contact | Hire an AI product builder | Send a qualified inquiry |

### 4. Create a short path from proof to payment

Every visitor should reach a relevant action within two decisions:

- Hiring team: proof -> resume -> role conversation.
- Consulting buyer: problem -> service -> scoped inquiry.
- Product buyer: case study -> live product/source -> commercial inquiry.
- Partner or acquirer: product evidence -> partnership/acquisition inquiry.

Generic calls to action are secondary. Page-specific actions should use the visitor's language.

## Offer Architecture

### AI Product Opportunity Sprint

- Buyer: founder, product lead, innovation lead, or operating team with an expensive customer or workflow problem.
- Outcome: evidence-backed problem definition, product concept, workflow, requirements, technical approach, and implementation plan.
- Typical duration: 1-2 weeks.
- Starting range: $3,500-$7,500.
- Proof: customer-led problem framing and product decisions across the portfolio.

### AI Product Build

- Buyer: company ready to test a real workflow instead of another strategy document.
- Outcome: working MVP, deterministic core logic, AI-assisted workflow where useful, instrumentation, and pilot-ready handoff.
- Typical duration: 4-8 weeks.
- Starting range: $15,000-$40,000+ depending on integrations and risk.
- Proof: Treasury Router, GridSynapse, Orelis, and Monarch Doctor.

### Embedded AI Solutions Architect

- Buyer: crypto, fintech, or AI company that needs a customer-facing builder across discovery, solution design, prototypes, integrations, and adoption.
- Outcome: product gaps converted into shipped solutions and clearer customer value.
- Typical structure: monthly engagement or full-time role.
- Consulting range: $10,000-$18,000 per month, scoped to availability and responsibility.

## Organic Search and Answer-Engine Foundation

### Technical requirements

- Unique title, description, canonical URL, H1, and social metadata per public page.
- Crawlable HTML for the core answer, not text hidden behind interaction.
- `robots.txt` that permits intended public pages.
- XML sitemap with canonical public URLs.
- Internal links between home, services, resume, contact, and case studies.
- Person, ProfilePage, ProfessionalService, CreativeWork, and FAQ structured data only where the visible page supports it.
- Fast static delivery, responsive layouts, descriptive image alt text, and no broken assets.
- `llms.txt` as an additional machine-readable summary, not a substitute for normal search fundamentals.

### Editorial requirements

Every page should answer its target query in the opening section, then provide evidence. Use the terms hiring teams and buyers actually use where they are truthful:

- AI Solutions Architect
- AI Product Builder
- AI Product Consultant
- Forward-Deployed Product
- AI Agents
- Agentic AI
- x402 payments
- AI voice agent
- AI compute optimization
- crypto and fintech AI products

Do not repeat keywords mechanically. Search engines and buyers both reward clear, useful pages over keyword lists.

### Authority and citation loop

Each meaningful external profile should link to the most relevant permanent page:

- LinkedIn headline/about -> home, work, and resume.
- LinkedIn featured projects -> individual case studies.
- GitHub profile/readmes -> case study and live product.
- Upwork/Contra offers -> relevant service section and proof page.
- Product launch posts -> case study rather than a temporary social post.

This makes the site the canonical evidence layer while third-party profiles supply distribution and trust.

## Conversion System

### Inquiry qualification

Every inquiry should identify:

- Visitor type: hiring, product build, product use, partnership, or acquisition.
- Problem and affected user.
- Existing product or workflow.
- Desired outcome.
- Timeline.
- Budget or role scope where appropriate.

### Response standard

- Reply within one business day.
- For consulting, propose a 20-minute fit call or the Opportunity Sprint.
- For roles, map one or two case studies directly to the stated job.
- For product inquiries, clarify current functionality and commercial boundary before scheduling technical work.

## Measurement

### Weekly scorecard

- Search impressions and clicks by page and query.
- Branded versus non-branded search traffic.
- Case-study visits and outbound product/source clicks.
- Resume downloads.
- Contact actions by visitor path.
- Qualified hiring conversations.
- Qualified consulting inquiries.
- Product, partnership, licensing, and acquisition inquiries.
- Proposals sent, close rate, and booked revenue.

### Decision rules

- High impressions, low clicks: improve title and description.
- Traffic, low case-study engagement: improve opening problem and evidence.
- Case-study engagement, low inquiries: strengthen the relevant offer and CTA.
- Inquiries, low close rate: narrow buyer/problem fit and improve proof.
- A channel produces activity but no qualified conversations for eight weeks: reduce effort and reallocate.
