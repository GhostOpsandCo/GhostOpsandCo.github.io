## Gate

From June 2026 I worked on AI at Gate.

AI and Web3 in the same room. Front and center.

## Smart Routing

One thing we shipped: smart routing by Gate Router.

It picks the best model for each task automatically.

It typically saves 20 to 30% on token costs.

## Lighting Cash on Fire

Paying more for tokens doesn't mean more work gets done.

Most companies still send their simplest tasks to their most expensive models.

Your CFO and CTO see that as lighting cash on fire.

## Every Model Has a Lane

Speed. Cost. Context. Reasoning.

Every model is good at something different.

Routing means you don't have to pick by hand every time.

## In Closing

Work smarter, not harder.

That applies to models too. 🔥🤖
