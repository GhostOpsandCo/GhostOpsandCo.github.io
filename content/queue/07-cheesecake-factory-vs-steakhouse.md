## Two Kinds of Models

Frontier models know a little about everything.

Specialized models know one thing extremely well.

## The Analogy

The Cheesecake Factory has a massive menu, and everything on it is good.

Your favorite steakhouse serves one thing: the best steak in town.

## What I Saw

At companies like Gate I used both kinds on narrow problems where the results really mattered.

When the specialized model is within reach, it wins. Every time.

## Why It Matters for Trading

Products that help people make money in crypto are the most wanted, and the hardest to build.

That's where specialized models win.
